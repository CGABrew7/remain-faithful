# Remain Faithful 1.0.4 App Store description

Draft for the owner. Do not paste this into App Store Connect from this file until you have read it.

Remain Faithful is for covenant groups of up to 12 and for one partner you choose.

You pick the apps. Apple Screen Time keeps them shielded, including after you force quit Remain Faithful. Apple's adult web filter turns on with Blocking. A Partner PIN lets your partner hold the code for changes you both agreed to gate.

When a blocked app opens, the shield says Remain Faithful. Open Remain Faithful, then tap Tell my partner if you want them to know. That notice is the time and the words "A blocked app was opened." You can also send a daily check-in, with a status and a short note you write. An optional reminder opens the check-in screen, and you tap Send to deliver it.

Session Shield is optional. While it is on, your chosen apps stay shielded until you start Deep Scan from the system broadcast picker. iOS shows the recording indicator for the whole session. Stopping the broadcast, or the broadcast ending, shields those apps again. Classification runs on the phone. Your partner can receive a category, a severity, a confidence level, and the time. They also receive a notice when Deep Scan stops.

Family Controls is used for an individual managing their own phone. The in-app week view is there for you to look at with your partner beside you.

Remain Faithful is free. The source is public.
